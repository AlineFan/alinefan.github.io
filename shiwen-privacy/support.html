拾纹是一款从日常影像中发现纹样灵感的创作工具。

一张照片、一扇窗、一块布料，甚至一幅随手涂鸦，都可以成为图案创作的起点。拾纹帮助你将熟悉的素材转化为独特的纹样、图案，在变化与秩序之间探索新的视觉可能。

相册、相机和保存权限

权限或能力使用方式
照片选择器当你主动选择来源图片时使用。拾纹只接收你选中的图片，用于生成纹样。
相机当你点击拍照时请求，用于拍摄纹样来源图。拾纹不会在后台调用相机。
添加到照片当你点击下载或保存到相册时请求，用于将生成的图片写入系统照片图库。
系统分享当你点击分享时打开。作品只会发送到你选择的 App、联系人或服务。

你可以在 iOS“设置”中查看或撤销这些权限。撤销权限只会影响对应功能。

数据存储说明

  • 当前版本无需注册或登录。
  • 你选择的图片、生成的纹样和画廊项目默认保存在设备本地，不会上传到拾纹服务器。
  • 本地项目会保留到你在画廊中删除，或卸载 App。
  • 保存到系统照片图库或分享给其他服务的副本，需要在对应位置单独删除。
  • 如果设备启用了系统备份，Apple 可能依据你的 iCloud 和设备备份设置备份 App 数据。

English Version

Shiwen is a creative pattern design tool for discovering pattern inspiration in everyday images.

A photo, a window, a piece of fabric, or even a casual doodle can become the starting point for a new design. Shiwen helps transform familiar materials into unique motifs and patterns, letting you explore new visual possibilities through variation and order.

Photos, camera, and saving permissions

Permission or featureHow it is used
Photo pickerUsed when you choose a source image. Shiwen receives only the images you select and uses them to create patterns.
CameraRequested when you choose to take a source photo. Shiwen does not access the camera in the background.
Add to PhotosRequested when you download or save a creation to the system photo library.
System share sheetOpened when you choose Share. Your work is sent only to the app, person, or service you select.

You can review or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings. Revoking a permission affects only the related feature.

Data storage

  • The current version does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Selected images, generated patterns, and Gallery projects are stored locally on your device by default and are not uploaded to Shiwen servers.
  • Local projects remain until you delete them in Gallery or uninstall the app.
  • Copies saved to Photos or shared with another service must be deleted separately from those destinations.
  • If system backup is enabled, Apple may back up app data according to your iCloud and device backup settings.
