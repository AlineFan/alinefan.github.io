隐私政策

本政策说明拾纹（Shiwen Design）iOS App 当前版本如何处理图片、创作项目、系统权限、付费权益，以及接入的第三方服务。

创作仍是本地优先：无需注册，没有广告，也不会把你导入的图片、生成的纹样或本地项目上传到拾纹服务器。App 接入了 Apple、RevenueCat 和 PostHog，分别用于内购、权益校验和匿名产品分析。

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-08-19
适用版本 / Applies to: Shiwen iOS 1.1

中文版

1. 适用范围

本政策仅适用于拾纹（Shiwen Design）iOS App 当前公开版本。网站、未来的账号服务、云同步或云端纹样提取如有不同的数据处理方式，将在启用前另行说明并更新本政策。

2. 我们处理的数据

为了完成你主动发起的创作、购买或反馈，App 会处理以下内容：

  • 你选择或拍摄的图片：用于取样、生成万花筒和重复纹样。AI 高清处理在设备本地完成，不会把原图送到拾纹服务器。
  • 生成的纹样与本地项目：保存在 App 的本地存储中，并显示在画廊。
  • 语言、触感和音效偏好：保存在设备本地，用于下次打开 App 时保持设置。
  • 购买与 Pro 权益状态：通过 Apple 内购和 RevenueCat 校验，用于解锁风格导出、高清下载等付费功能。
  • 匿名产品事件：通过 PostHog 记录引导、付费墙、购买、保存、分享、下载和反馈等操作，用于改进产品。事件不绑定邮箱或 Apple ID。

图片和纹样由 App 在本机处理，不会上传给拾纹，也不会用来建立可识别的用户档案、投放广告或跨 App/网站追踪你。

3. 系统权限

权限或能力使用方式
照片选择器只有在你主动选择图片时使用；App 只接收你选中的项目。
相机只有在你点击拍照时请求，用于创建来源图。
添加到照片只有在你点击下载或保存到相册时请求，用于写入生成的图片。

你可以在 iOS“设置”中查看或撤销权限。撤销权限可能会让对应功能无法使用，但不会影响不依赖该权限的本地创作。

4. 账号、联网、分析与广告

  • 当前版本无需创建账号或登录，也不启用云同步。
  • 导入的图片、生成的纹样和画廊项目不会发送到拾纹服务器。
  • 没有广告，不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不做跨 App 或跨网站追踪。
  • App 会联网以完成 App Store 内购、RevenueCat 权益校验，以及 PostHog 匿名产品分析。

6. 保存、删除与备份

  • 来源图片副本和纹样项目会保留在 App 本地，直到你在画廊中删除或卸载 App。
  • 已保存到系统照片图库或发送到其他服务的副本，需要在相应位置单独删除。
  • 如果设备启用了系统备份，App 数据可能由 Apple 按你的 iCloud 与设备备份设置进行备份。

由于当前版本没有用户账号或云端项目库，我们无法从服务器读取或删除你的本地作品。你可以直接在 App 内删除，或卸载 App 清除其本地数据。

7. 儿童隐私

拾纹是一款通用设计工具，不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标用户。当前版本不通过服务器主动收集儿童或其他用户的个人信息。

8. 政策更新

当 App 增加账号、云同步、云端提取，或更换、新增会改变数据处理方式的第三方服务时，我们会在相应功能启用前更新本页面，并调整生效日期。重大变化会尽量在 App 内提示。

9. 联系我们

如对本政策或 App 的数据处理有疑问，请发送邮件至 yousabrina@me.com。请勿在邮件中附上不必要的私人照片、身份信息或其他敏感内容。

English Version

1. Scope

This policy applies to the current public version of the Shiwen Design iOS app. If the website or future account, cloud sync, or cloud-based Extraction features process data differently, we will explain those practices and update this policy before they are enabled.

2. Data processed by the app

To perform creation, purchase, or feedback actions you request, the app processes:

  • Images you select or capture: used as source material for sampling, kaleidoscope rendering, and repeat-pattern creation. AI HD enhancement runs on-device and does not send the original image to Shiwen servers.
  • Generated patterns and local projects: stored locally by the app and displayed in Gallery.
  • Language, haptics, and sound preferences: stored on-device so the app can remember your settings.
  • Purchase and Pro entitlement status: checked through Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat to unlock paid features such as styled export and HD download.
  • Anonymous product events: recorded with PostHog for onboarding, paywall, purchase, save, share, download, and feedback actions, so we can improve the product. Events are not tied to an email address or Apple ID.

Images and patterns are processed on-device. They are not uploaded to Shiwen, used for advertising, used to build an identifiable user profile, or used to track you across apps or websites.

3. System permissions and features

Permission or featureHow it is used
Photo pickerUsed only when you choose an image. The app receives only the items you select.
CameraRequested only when you choose to take a photo for use as source material.
Add to PhotosRequested only when you download or save a generated image to your photo library.
System share sheetOpened only when you choose Share. Content is sent only to the app or service you select.

You can review or revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Revoking a permission may disable the related feature, but it does not prevent other local creation features from working.

4. Accounts, network use, analytics, and advertising

  • The current version does not require an account or sign-in, and does not enable cloud sync.
  • Imported images, generated patterns, and Gallery projects are not sent to Shiwen servers.
  • There are no ads, no IDFA, and no cross-app or cross-website tracking.
  • The app uses the network for App Store purchases, RevenueCat entitlement checks, and anonymous PostHog product analytics.

6. Storage, deletion, and backups

  • Source-image copies and pattern projects remain in the app's local storage until you delete them in Gallery or uninstall the app.
  • Copies saved to Photos or sent to another service must be deleted separately from those destinations.
  • If system backup is enabled, Apple may back up app data according to your iCloud and device backup settings.

Because the current version has no user account or cloud project library, we cannot access or delete your local projects from a server. You can delete them directly in the app or remove the app and its local data.

7. Children's privacy

Shiwen is a general-purpose design tool and is not directed to children under 13. The current version does not actively collect personal information from children or other users through our servers.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app adds accounts, cloud sync, cloud-based Extraction, or replaces or adds a third-party service that changes how data is handled, we will update this page before enabling that processing and revise the effective date. We will aim to provide an in-app notice for material changes.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app's data practices, email yousabrina@me.com. Do not attach unnecessary private photos, identity information, or other sensitive content.
