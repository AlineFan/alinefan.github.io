Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

VoiceCue is designed with a simple principle: what you record is yours alone. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. VoiceCue does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, analytics, or telemetry.

Local data

The app stores the following on your device only, never leaving it:

  • Your scripts and the speech the app generates from them
  • Your imported and recorded reference audio
  • Your video recordings, before you export them to Photos
  • Your preferences (playback speed, aspect ratio, voice selection)

These are saved inside the app's sandbox and removed when you uninstall VoiceCue.

Device permissions

VoiceCue asks for the following iOS permissions, purely for on-device work:

  • Camera — to record you through the front-facing lens. Saved locally, never uploaded.
  • Microphone — to record your voice into the video, and to record reference audio. Saved locally, never uploaded.
  • Photo Library (Add Only) — to save your finished videos into the Photos app. VoiceCue cannot read photos or videos that are already in your library.

Network access

VoiceCue works fully offline. Text-to-speech runs on your iPhone via Apple's AVSpeechSynthesizer. No audio, video, or text is sent to any server.

Third-party services

None. VoiceCue does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

Children

VoiceCue is safe for all ages. Because no data is collected, no special provisions are needed for users under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect it.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 4 月 24 日

VoiceCue 的原则只有一条：你录下的东西只属于你。应用不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人信息。

我们收集哪些数据

一条都不收。VoiceCue 不会收集、存储或上传任何个人数据、使用数据、分析数据或遥测数据。

本地数据

以下内容只保存在你自己的设备上，永远不会离开：

  • 你的文稿，以及应用根据文稿生成的朗读音频
  • 你导入和录制的参考音频
  • 导出到相册之前的视频文件
  • 你的偏好设置（播放速度、画面比例、朗读音色）

这些内容保存在应用的沙盒里，卸载 VoiceCue 时会一并清除。

设备权限

VoiceCue 会申请下面这几项 iOS 权限，全部用于设备本地功能：

  • 相机：通过前置镜头录你的视频。只保存在本地，不会上传。
  • 麦克风：录制你在视频里的声音，以及录制参考音频。只保存在本地，不会上传。
  • 相册（仅添加）：把做完的视频保存到相册。VoiceCue 无法读取你已有的照片或视频。

网络访问

VoiceCue 完全离线运行。文本转语音由 iPhone 本地的 Apple AVSpeechSynthesizer 完成。任何音频、视频、文字都不会被发到任何服务器。

第三方服务

没有。VoiceCue 不使用任何第三方分析、广告、崩溃统计或追踪服务。

儿童隐私

VoiceCue 适合所有年龄段。因为应用不收集任何数据，所以不需要为 13 岁以下用户做特别处理。

Support

Will the reference audio end up in my video?

No. The reference plays only in your headphones. The microphone captures your voice alone. The one thing to remember: always wear headphones.

What headphones should I use?

Wired earphones give zero latency and the best feel. AirPods work well too. Other Bluetooth headphones may introduce slight playback lag — it can nudge your rhythm but it never contaminates the recording.

Can I record without headphones?

Technically yes, but the reference audio from the speaker will bleed into the microphone and muddy your recording. Headphones are the whole point — don't skip them.

How do I prepare a reference audio?

Three ways, depending on what's easiest for you:

  • Record it yourself. Read the script into the app once, and use that take as your reference.
  • Import a file. Pull in any mp3, m4a, or wav from your phone.
  • Let the app read it. Type your script and VoiceCue generates speech on-device using a system voice.

Where are my videos saved?

When you tap Save to Photos on the preview screen, the video goes to the iOS Photos app. You can also reopen recent takes from the app's library without saving them to Photos first.

Can I change playback speed or aspect ratio?

Yes. Speed adjusts from 0.7× to 1.3× so you can slow a fast reference down or push it a little. Aspect ratio offers 9:16 (portrait, for Reels / TikTok / Shorts), 3:4, and 1:1.

Does VoiceCue need internet?

No. Every feature — including text-to-speech — runs on your iPhone.

帮助

参考音会被录进我的视频吗？

不会。参考音只在耳机里播放。麦克风只录你自己的声音。唯一要记住的事：戴耳机。

用什么耳机最好？

有线耳机零延迟，体验最好。AirPods 也可以。其他蓝牙耳机可能有轻微播放延迟，会影响你跟读的节奏，但不会影响录音质量。

不戴耳机能录吗？

技术上能，但外放的参考音会被麦克风录进去，成片会很混乱。耳机是这个方法的核心，别省。

怎么准备参考音？

三种方式，选你最方便的那一种：

  • 自己录一遍。对着应用读一遍稿子，拿这一遍当参考。
  • 从手机导入。任何 mp3、m4a、wav 文件都能用。
  • 让应用替你读。输入文稿，VoiceCue 在本地用系统朗读音色合成参考音。

录好的视频存在哪？

在预览页点保存到相册，视频会写入 iOS 相册。没保存也没关系，可以在应用的我的录音里重新打开最近录的视频。

能调播放速度和画面比例吗？

可以。速度在 0.7× 到 1.3× 之间调节，可以把语速快的参考音慢下来，或者稍微推快一点。画面比例支持 9:16（竖屏，适合小红书、抖音、TikTok、Reels、Shorts）、3:4、1:1。

VoiceCue 需要联网吗？

不需要。所有功能，包括文本转语音，都在 iPhone 本地运行。
